## Full Description (max ~4000 chars)

VibeMsg is a next-generation messaging app that puts privacy, creativity, and real-time communication at your fingertips.

**🔐 Privacy First**
Every chat can be protected with AES-256 end-to-end encryption — your keys, your messages. Lock individual chats with a 4–6 digit passcode backed by Face ID / biometrics. Screen capture shield prevents screenshots of your conversations.

**🎵 Musical Messages**
Send messages that pulse to a beat. Attach music to any message — the bubble animates to the rhythm when your contact opens it.

**📸 Secure Picture Messages**
Send photos with "view once", "timed" (auto-expires), or "password-protected" modes. Gone after viewing — no screenshots possible.

**📡 Real-Time Server Messaging**
Connect with anyone on VibeMsg via username. Real-time group chats, read receipts, typing indicators, and online presence.

**📣 Broadcast Check-In Groups**
Create check-in groups for teams or families. Members broadcast their status — everyone sees who's checked in at a glance.

**📞 Voice & Video Calls**
High-quality voice and video calling built right in. Call history, missed call notifications, and do-not-disturb support.

**🎨 Skin Store**
Customize your chat bubble style with AI-generated skins. Purchase premium skins with VibeCoin.

**🔔 Smart Notifications**
Per-chat notification sounds (6 options), mute individual chats, custom sound per conversation, and read receipt control.

**📋 PDF Export**
Export any chat as a beautifully formatted PDF — great for records, legal use, or memories.

**👥 Contact Groups & Card Sharing**
Organize contacts into groups. Share your contact card as a live widget others can save instantly.

**🔍 Advanced Search**
Full-text search across all your messages, with filters by date, sender, and media type.
