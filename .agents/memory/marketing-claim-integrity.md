---
name: Marketing claim integrity
description: Rules for keeping ZIVR public-facing product and security messaging aligned with verified behavior.
---

Public ZIVR messaging must be reviewed against the current mobile implementation, not copied wholesale from store metadata, roadmap items, or UI labels.

**Why:** Product wording can get ahead of reality. In particular, contact controls, call infrastructure, encryption/biometric claims, and backup protection have distinct implementation limits that should not be represented as stronger guarantees in public materials.

**How to apply:** Before publishing website, deck, store, or launch copy, validate each behavioral and security claim in code. Use qualified, implementation-accurate language where a screen or control exists without the underlying protection or network behavior being complete.

Static product mockups that use invented people, timestamps, statuses, counts, or activity must visibly say “Sample” or “Preview” inside the mockup itself.

**Why:** Surrounding marketing copy does not reliably qualify what a screenshot-like interface appears to prove; unlabeled sample data can be read as real operational activity.

**How to apply:** Put the label in the mockup header or primary content area, remove unnecessary fake metrics, and separately disclose when a screen is only a visual preview of functionality that is not live.
